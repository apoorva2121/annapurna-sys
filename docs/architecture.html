01 Two apps, one deployable

Employees and customers are separated by structure, not by a conditional. A guest request cannot enter staff routing, because there is no path from one route tree to the other.

Why the duplication is the feature. Staff and guest responses use separate serialisers, so some shaping is written twice. That is deliberate: a field added for staff cannot silently appear in a guest response, and guest payloads carry no cost, margin, recipe or employee name.

02 The path of a request

Four layers, each forbidden from doing the next one's job. This single rule is why tenant isolation and permission coverage can be tested generically instead of endpoint by endpoint.

04 Recipes nest, cost rolls up

An Indian kitchen is base-driven. The morning prep is eight litres of makhani gravy that feeds six dishes — so a recipe line points at an ingredient or at another preparation, and cost rolls upward through the nesting.
