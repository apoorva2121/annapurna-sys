| Staff without a login cannot see their own performance score | Owner question, 2026-09-14 | ADR 0011 §3 promises that **every employee can always see their own score and its inputs** (`performance.view.own` on every role, not removable). ADR 0007 makes an employee without a login normal — in the seed, two kitchen staff and both cleaners. Those people can be measured (shifts, tasks, wastage reference them) but have no way to open the app, so the guarantee silently fails for the lowest-paid staff it exists to protect. **Decide before M17 starts.** Options: (a) a view-only login that shows only the person's own score and shifts, and nothing else; (b) a periodic SMS or WhatsApp summary of their own score, which needs their consent, a messaging channel (out of scope for month 1) and a rule for a shared or missing phone; (c) a summary a manager must show them, recorded as shown and acknowledged — cheapest, and the hardest to prove happened. Whichever is chosen, a score for someone with no way to see it should not be published or used for incentives. See `docs/workload-fairness.md` and `docs/decisions/0011-performance-measurement.md`. |
