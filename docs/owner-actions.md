**Generated into `Apoorva-actions-and-questions.xlsx` by `scripts/build_owner_workbook.py`.** This
file is the source of truth; the workbook is a view of it. Edit here, rebuild, and nothing is lost.

Two lists, and they are different in kind:

- **Actions** are things only you can do — a console session, a payment method, a phone in your
  hand, a conversation with the pilot restaurant. None of them is blocked on me.
- **Questions** are decisions I should not make alone. Each one names the options, says which I
  would choose and why, and says what it costs to leave it open. **Leaving one open is a valid
  answer** for most of them; two are marked where it is not.

Everything here traces to a file in this repository. Nothing is invented for the sake of a row.
